CROSS-PROCESS STABILITY: the refuse policy was run TWICE, in two separate
processes (5m28s and 6m59s wall), and produced all three hashes identically.
That is the pin: same seed + same policy -> same campaign, across processes.

WHAT THE THREE POLICIES MEASURE (same seed, same two asks — escort weight 9 on
day 3, arbitration weight 7 nine days later):
  refuse  both refused; the abbey's ledger stamped -7.00 on day 12 and read
          +0.00 on day 36 (24 days x UP_PER_DAY 0.5, clamped at 0), while the
          two rivals — pleased by the refusals — stamped +11.25 and read +5.25
          (24 days x DOWN_PER_DAY 0.25). The exact 2.0 asymmetry, in a live
          campaign.
  lapse   the SAME two asks cost 13.50 and 10.50 standing (= 9 x 1.5 and
          7 x 1.5 — silence is 1.5x a refusal) AND bought nothing with the
          rivals, who stayed stamped at their founding +10.00 and drifted down
          to +1.25. Silence is worse than a refusal twice over.
  send    day 3: four named companions rode out with 16 rations, were away from
          colony work for two days, came home, and the abbey paid full credit
          (renown 5 = round(weight 9 x share 1.0 x 0.5)). The SECOND ask could
          not be sent on six consecutive days — "the larder cannot spare 4 —
          there is 0" — and lapsed on day 16. The squeeze, stated by the sim.

NOT REACHED BY ANY 35-DAY RUN: the hostility latch (standing <= -60). Two asks
in 35 days can cost at most 24 standing, so no organic run of this length can
get there; the latch and its two doors (tribute paid / beating the raid they
sent) stay pinned by `webband_app`'s
`hostility_latches_and_has_exactly_two_doors`, not by this soak. Stated rather
than dressed up.
